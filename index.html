§ 01 — Why Apex

A builder who puts it in writing.

Most jobs go wrong at the quoting stage, not on site. Everything we do is designed to take the guesswork out before anyone lifts a tool.

A

Local & accountable

Based in Trowbridge, working within a few miles of it. The person who quotes your job is the person accountable for it at handover.

B

Fixed price, plain English

A written, line-by-line quote — not a phone-call estimate. No hidden charges, no mid-job surprises. The number we agree is the number you pay.

C

Planning & building control

We prepare the applications and book the inspections with Wiltshire, B&NES and Somerset, so approvals don't stall the programme.

D

Tidy sites, every day

Skip on the drive, dust sheets down, end-of-day sweep. You carry on living in the house while we work in it — that's the part customers remember.

§ 03 — How we work

Three steps. No surprises.

Every project follows the same three-step rhythm — whether it's a small bathroom or a double-storey extension. The clarity is the point.

01

Free site visit

We come out, measure up, listen properly, and explain what's possible — and what isn't — within your budget and the local planning rules.

02

Written quote

A fixed-price quote in plain English, broken down line by line. Drawings if needed. Time to think — no pressure to sign on the spot.

03

Build & handover

One named foreman, one schedule, one number to call. We finish on the agreed date with the site cleaner than we found it.
