§ How we quote

Everything in writing, before we start.

Most jobs go wrong at the quoting stage, not on site. So we don't do phone-call estimates. We come out, measure up, talk through what's actually possible, and then put a written, line-by-line quote in your hand.

It takes us longer. It pays back in trust. You can see exactly what's included, compare it properly against anyone else's, and change the spec before it costs anything to change. The number we agree is the number you pay.

If a builder won't put it in writing, you're at the wrong builder.

§ Credentials

What we're verified for.

A

You deal with the trader

Apex Construction is a sole trader based in Trowbridge. The person who quotes the job is accountable for it at handover — no account manager, no chain to chase.

B

Independently reviewed

Reviews are collected from homeowners after handover and published unedited. Read the live feed on our reviews page.

C

Public liability insured

Full public liability and employer's liability cover. Documents available on request before any contract is signed.

Every structural job is inspected and signed off by the relevant local authority building control team. You get the certificates at handover.
