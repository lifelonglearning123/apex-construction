Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 April 2026

This privacy policy explains how Apex Construction ("Apex", "we", "us") collects and uses personal data when you visit our website or contact us. Apex Construction is a sole trader and is the data controller for the personal data described here. Our business address is 30 Cavell Court, Trowbridge, Wiltshire, BA14 0HH.

1. What we collect

When you submit our contact form or email us, we collect: name, phone number, email address, postcode, and the project details you provide. When you visit the website we collect basic technical information (IP address, browser type, pages viewed) for analytics purposes.

2. Why we collect it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a site visit
  • To provide a written quote for the work you've described
  • To manage the project if you proceed with us
  • To improve our website and service (anonymised analytics only)

3. Lawful basis

We process your data on the basis of: (a) your consent when you submit our contact form; (b) legitimate interests in responding to enquiries and providing quotes; (c) contract when you become a customer; and (d) legal obligation for tax records and insurance purposes.

4. How long we keep it

Enquiry data: 24 months from last contact, then deleted unless you become a customer. Customer data: 7 years from project completion (HMRC requirement). Analytics data: anonymised after 26 months.

5. Who we share it with

We don't sell your data, ever. We may share necessary information with: (a) Wiltshire Council or B&NES when submitting planning or building-regs applications on your behalf; (b) HMRC for tax purposes; (c) our website analytics provider in anonymised form. All third parties are GDPR-compliant.

6. Cookies

This website uses essential cookies to function and may use anonymised analytics cookies (Google Analytics or similar). You can disable cookies in your browser settings. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to: access the data we hold on you, correct it, request deletion, restrict processing, object to processing, and lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). To exercise any of these, email apexconstructionsouth@gmail.com.

8. Security

We store enquiry data in secure email and CRM systems with access restricted to Apex staff. Customer files are kept in our office in lockable cabinets. We don't process payment card data on our website.

9. ICO complaint

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk, or call 0303 123 1113.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
