How Much Does a House Extension Cost in Wiltshire? 2026 Price Guide.

Real per-square-metre figures, the eight cost variables most quotes hide, and three honest worked examples — from the Trowbridge builder that runs 30+ extensions a year across Wiltshire and Somerset.

Most cost guides give you a wide per-m² range and leave it at that. This one is going to do better. We'll show you what the headline numbers cover, what they don't, and the eight variables that move a £50k extension to £75k or £40k.

Everything below is based on jobs we actually price and build in Wiltshire and Somerset — not industry averages from a magazine.

The headline 2026 figures

For standard-spec extensions in Wiltshire, on accessible sites with normal soil conditions, here's what to budget per square metre of new floor area:

  • Single-storey rear extension — £2,200 to £2,800 per m². A 24m² kitchen-extension lands at £53k–£67k.
  • Double-storey rear or side extension — £1,800 to £2,400 per m² of total new floor area (the per-m² rate drops because foundations and roof are spread across two floors). A 40m² double-storey is typically £72k–£96k.
  • Wraparound extension (rear + side L-shape) — £2,400 to £3,200 per m² because of the more complex roof and corner detailing. A 50m² wrap is typically £120k–£160k.
  • Side return / side infill on a Victorian terrace — £2,500 to £3,200 per m². Smaller footprint but disproportionately complex.
What "standard spec" means here

uPVC or aluminium windows, decent ceramic floor tiles, mid-market fitted kitchen (Howdens or similar), one set of bi-fold or French doors, plasterboard finish, painted internally, brick-clad to match the existing house. No structural surprises. Roughly the spec 70% of our Wiltshire clients choose.

The 8 variables that move the number

  1. Foundations. Heavy clay across most of Wiltshire means 1.0–1.2m strip footings, sometimes deeper near mature trees. Heave from clay shrinkage adds £1,500–£3,000 vs sandy soils.
  2. Bi-folds vs French doors vs sliders. A 4m bi-fold runs £6k–£10k installed. French doors are £1.5k–£3k. Aluminium sliders run £8k–£14k. Easily a £10k swing.
  3. Roof type. Flat warm roof: cheapest. Pitched tiled roof: +£3k–£6k. Pitched with rooflights or lantern: +£5k–£12k. Vaulted with structural beams: +£8k–£20k.
  4. Kitchen spec. Howdens trade kitchen (£8k–£14k installed) vs Wren mid-tier (£18k–£28k) vs bespoke local cabinet maker (£35k–£70k). Single biggest discretionary cost.
  5. Floor finish. Decent porcelain at £40/m² fitted vs large-format porcelain at £100+/m² fitted. On 30m² that's a £1.8k swing — minimum.
  6. Underfloor heating. Wet UFH on top of insulation: +£1,800–£3,500 plus a manifold and zone valves.
  7. Listed-building / conservation work. Bath stone matching, lime mortar, sash window profile-matching: +20–40% on shell costs.
  8. Access & logistics. Properties without rear-garden vehicle access need crane lifts, hand-barrow runs, or smaller skip swaps. Adds £3k–£8k. Common in central Bradford-on-Avon and parts of Bath.

What's not in the headline figure

This is where most quotes get sneaky. Always check whether the per-m² number includes:

  • Architect / drawings — typically £2k–£5k. We provide drawings in-house, others charge separately.
  • Planning fees — householder application £258 in 2026; pre-app advice from Wiltshire Council typically £180.
  • Building Control fees — £600–£1,200 depending on project size.
  • Structural engineer's calcs — £400–£1,200.
  • Party Wall surveyor (if applicable) — £700–£1,500 per affected neighbour.
  • VAT — most domestic extensions are 20% VAT-rated. A £50k quote is £60k all-in.
  • Skip hire and welfare — should be included. Sometimes isn't.
  • Final decoration — fully painted internally? Or "ready to decorate"?

The cleanest comparison between two builders is to ask both for a single all-in figure that includes drawings, planning, BC, calcs, decoration and VAT. If they hedge, that's a flag.

Three worked examples

Example 1 — 24m² single-storey rear, Trowbridge BA14

1930s semi. Permitted development. Standard spec (Howdens kitchen, bi-fold, flat warm roof, porcelain floor, ceiling speakers). 11 weeks on site. £58,000 ex-VAT.

Example 2 — 48m² two-storey side, Lansdown Bath BA1

Conservation area, full planning. Bath stone-match shell, sash windows, en-suite upstairs. 19 weeks. £128,000 ex-VAT.

Example 3 — 64m² wraparound, Bradford-on-Avon BA15

Listed Grade II terrace. LBC + planning. Lime mortar, hand-cut stone match, sash windows, bespoke joinery. 22 weeks. £195,000 ex-VAT.

"Cost per square metre" is a useful starting point, not a quote. The quote is what you sign — and it should be a fixed total, in writing, with line items. If a builder won't put a fixed figure on paper, walk away.
